Welcome to your course

Follow the project's instructions to complete the activity's Tasks.

To grade a single Task, select the greyed-out square next to each task.

To check your code, click the "Build" button and select the HTML file you would like to view.

By design, the "Build" button will launch your website in a browser tab.

Select the down toggle arrow to the right of each Task to see feedback on any failed tests.

You can continue to modify, run, and calculate your grade until you're ready to submit.

Select "Submit" to turn in your assignment to your instructor and submit your grade to MindTap.
