How can I determine if a baby animal is an orphan?

Observe the baby animal for several hours. If the mother does not return, contact a wildlife rehabilitator.

How can I tell if an animal has rabies?

Signs of rabies include unusual behavior, such as aggression, excessive drooling, difficulty walking, and daytime activity in nocturnal animals. Do not approach the animal. Contact animal control immediately.

I found a baby bird that fell from its nest. Will the parents orphan it if I touch it?

No, most birds have a limited sense of smell and will not abandon their young because of human scent. However, it is best to place the bird back in the nest if possible and leave the area to allow the parents to return.
